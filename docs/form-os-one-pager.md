## Who should buy Unlimited

Choose **Unlimited ($299 one-time)** when your **company** runs **two or more** commercial products or SaaS apps on Flex Fields. One legal entity, every product covered—production, staging, and CI included.

Choose **Single Product ($169)** for one named SaaS or app.

Choose **Custom (from $1,500)** only if you **redistribute Flex Fields as a kit** (starter templates, white-label field pack, competing UI library). Unlimited does **not** include kit rights.

## Form OS vs “a few nice fields”

- **Standalone fields** — drop a `PhoneField` into any Filament form; no platform commitment.
- **Form OS** — global density/theme, schema registry, certified layout recipes (Translatable × SegmentTabs × ItemCard), quality gates, and Playwright coverage across hub groups.

**Flex Forms** (commercial companion) adds Studio, public fill, embeds, and Insights on top of Flex Fields—ideal when forms are your product surface, not just admin CRUD.
